# Roadmap

Phase 1 ends after eight validated checkpoints: repository/design; data/seed; auth; RBAC; counsellor services; student UI; support/admin shells; integration audit.
Recommended Phase 2, only after review: institutional programme/version publishing, enrolment and counsellor assignment workflows; persisted timetable/class sessions and lesson progress; resources and deterministic evidence collection. Specify audit/retention policy and administrative role provisioning first.
Later: mastery validation/history, assignments and controlled assessments, registrar corrections, notifications and deterministic support triggers. AI actors come only after permissioned APIs and technical activity firewalls.
Before production: staging Auth/RLS/provider tests, SMTP, operational backups, rate limits/abuse controls, privacy policy, support data governance, accessibility/browser/device testing and security review. Do not confuse a passing local build with operational readiness.

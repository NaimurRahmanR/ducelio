# Product principles

Ducelio is a digital academy preparing learners for external examinations. It is not an awarding body, LMS marketplace or chatbot. Programme examples do not define the reusable domain.
Institutional records are owned by deterministic backend services. Learner continuity is represented by a persistent counsellor assignment, independent of enrolment in a particular programme.
Phase 1 establishes identity, academic structure, support relationships and usable student/staff shells. No LLM APIs, agents, payments, full mastery engine or assessment delivery engine.
All demo content is illustrative and unvalidated. Do not label the demo blueprint as an official ISC2 syllabus.

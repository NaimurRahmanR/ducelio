# Future academic actor boundary
No AI integrations are implemented in Phase 1.
Future actors are external callers of narrowly scoped application services. Never issue database credentials or service-role tokens to them. Actor capabilities are bounded by institution, learner relationship, programme version, activity state and a specific operation.
Actors may submit LearningEvidence; deterministic validation owns mastery. They cannot overwrite grades, attendance, enrolment, deadlines or official records. Require auditable provenance, idempotency, rate limits and human review where policy calls for it.
The counsellor relationship remains institutional even if a future interface is conversational. Assessment and homework firewalls are enforced before retrieval and mutation, not by conversational instructions.

# Counsellor architecture
CounsellorAssignment is a persistent learner relationship, independent of programme enrolment. Assignments are staff-role validated, dated and history-preserving. Reassignment ends the current relationship and creates a new one in an audited transaction.
StudentSupportProfile stores study goals, preferred contact and learning availability, not speculative sensitive profiling. Study plans organise learning; due dates on personal plan items do not modify institutional deadlines.
SupportEvent stores deterministic condition type, source reference, occurrence time and deduplication key. Event generation belongs to backend detection workflows, not client input. No automated intervention engine is implemented.
Current service boundaries: getStudentSupportProfile, getStudyPlan, getOpenSupportEvents, createStudyPlanItem, createSupportFollowup, recordCounsellorInteraction and assignCounsellor. Future programme/deadline/weak-objective queries must use their owning services, not unrestricted raw DB access.
Learners read their own profile, plans and learner-visible follow-ups. Assigned counsellors access permitted context. Private interaction summaries are excluded from student views and inaccessible to instructors/admins absent an explicit support assignment and role. Reassignment access follows current assignment; introduce a reviewed historical-access policy before broader case transfer.
Do not store health diagnoses or other unnecessary sensitive narrative. Private note retention, access/export and escalation procedures require an institutional policy before real counselling use.
